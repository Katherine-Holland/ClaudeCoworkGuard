Features

Everything a security team
actually needs.

Built on the same local-first proxy as the free version — with a team layer on top.

Skill Registry + Quarantine
Central approved list of AI skills. Anything not on the list is quarantined before execution. Security team approves or blocks from the Shield dashboard.
Slack & Webhook Alerts
Real-time notifications when a skill is quarantined, a critical payload is blocked, or a developer navigates to a sensitive domain while an AI session is active.
Centralised Audit Dashboard
Aggregate view across every developer machine. See which AI tools are sending the most data, which patterns are triggering, and who is doing what — without seeing raw content.
Compliance Export
Generate PDF and CSV audit reports for any date range. SOC 2 and ISO 27001 friendly format. Shows what was blocked, flagged, and approved — ready for your auditors.
Policy Management
Admin sets blocking rules for the whole team. Developers can't override them. Critical findings are always blocked — no individual can turn that off.
Local-first by design
Raw payload content never leaves the developer's machine. Shield receives only hashes, counts, and finding types — never the actual data that triggered a detection.
